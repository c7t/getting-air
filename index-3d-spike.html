3D LBM register/spill spike — D3Q19 vs D3Q27 (plans/3D.md, M0)

Dense periodic grid, no body, no AMR, no rendering. Three kernel modes with identical memory traffic (Q planes in, Q planes out per cell), so the columns are comparable in GB/s: full = pull-stream + BGK collide, stream = gather and write back (the access-pattern ceiling), collide = own-cell read + collide (same registers, coalesced addresses). Compare GB/s, not GLUPS: GLUPS is expected to fall by 27/19 between the velocity sets. GB/s falling — especially collide falling at Q27 while stream holds — is the register-spill signature.
